## 2. Cannibalization Guard Rules (K05)

1. **One Canonical URL per Intent**:
   - The phrase `boat ride naivasha`, `lake naivasha boat ride`, and `boat rides in naivasha` are satisfied by the `/boat-rides/` hub and `/` homepage.
   - Do NOT create separate doorway pages for minor prepositional variations (`boat-ride-at-lake-naivasha`, `boat-ride-in-naivasha`).
2. **Distinct Sanctuary vs Boat Pricing**:
   - `crescent island entrance fee` queries land on `/crescent-island/`, which clearly delineates external sanctuary fees from Mosety's boat transfer rates.
3. **Internal Anchor Text Discipline**:
   - Use descriptive, exact anchors:
     - `Lake Naivasha boat rides` &rarr; links `/boat-rides/`
     - `transparent boat ride prices` &rarr; links `/prices/`
     - `Crescent Island boat trip` &rarr; links `/boat-rides/crescent-island/`
     - `Karagita public beach directions` &rarr; links `/contact/`
