How to Reach Karagita Pier

1. Driving from Nairobi via Mai Mahiu (Scenic Route ~1.5 - 2 Hours)

Take the lower Rift Valley escarpment road (B3 via Mai Mahiu). As you approach Naivasha town, turn left onto Moi South Lake Road. Continue along South Lake Road for approximately 8 kilometers until you reach the Karagita junction. Follow the signed beach access road down to the public boat pier.

2. Driving from Nairobi via Flyover / Naivasha Town (~2 Hours)

Take the upper dual carriageway (A104). Turn into Naivasha Town at the main roundabout and branch south onto Moi South Lake Road towards Hell's Gate and Karagita. The beach pier is an easy 10-minute drive from town center.

3. Public Transport (Matatu from Nairobi)

Board a Naivasha-bound matatu from the Nairobi CBD (Accra Road / Railways terminals). At the Naivasha main stage, board a local Karagita-bound matatu (KES 50–70) and alight at the Karagita Beach stage, followed by a short 3-minute walk to the boat pier.
