Our Lake Lineage

Built on Lake Naivasha Waters

Mosety Boat Rides Naivasha was founded with a simple, resolute conviction: Lake Naivasha is best experienced directly through the eyes, navigation skills, and conservation values of the local captains who grew up on its shores.

The Problem with Lake Tourism

For years, visiting Lake Naivasha has been complicated by middleman brokers, aggressive touts at highway junctions, and commission layers that inflate prices while shortchanging boat captains.

Worse still, commission-driven operations frequently rush passengers or crowd hippo pods dangerously close just to satisfy quick photo demands, compromising passenger safety and stressing the lake’s wild inhabitants.

Mosety provides an ethical, transparent alternative: direct access to certified local captains, published dock rates with zero hidden markups, and unhurried wildlife watching governed by strict maritime safety standards.

Our Four Foundational Commitments

01

Direct Pier Pricing

Standard published dock tariffs. No broker markups, no tourist surcharges, and no surprise dock fees upon arrival.

02

Tested Safety Gear

Mandatory fitted life jackets for every passenger, including certified vests for infants and small children.

03

Ethical 30–50m Buffers

We respect wildlife. We cut engines and drift quietly, never harassing hippo pods or crowding birds off their nests.

04

Local Stewardship

100% of charter income directly supports local boat captain families and conservation of Lake Naivasha's papyrus shorelines.
