Visiting Lake Naivasha with Children: A Parent's Guide

A boat safari on Lake Naivasha is one of the most enriching wildlife experiences a child can have in Kenya—watching wild hippos surface with a blow of water spray, spotting pelican flocks taking flight, and walking alongside towering giraffes on Crescent Island. Here is how Mosety ensures family trips are calm, safe, and stress-free for parents.

Certified Infant & Toddler Life Jackets

Unlike many informal dock operators who only carry adult-sized vests, Mosety stocks dedicated commercial infant life vests (with buoyant head support collars and crotch straps for under 15kg) and child-sized vests (15–35kg). Your captain personally fits and tests every strap before your child steps onto the boat.

Gentle Boarding & Steady Craft

Our wide-beam fiberglass safari vessels feature stable hulls that minimize rolling. Your captain assists each child one-by-one during boarding and seating. We position younger children in sheltered central bench seats where they can comfortably see the water without leaning out.

Pacing for Curious Minds

Family safaris are never rushed. Captains slow down to point out baby hippos swimming close to their mothers, demonstrate how water lily leaves repel water droplets, and let kids watch fish eagles swoop down from yellow acacia branches.

Practical Tips for Parents

Stroller vs. Baby Carrier: While strollers can be stored on the boat during your ride, Crescent Island consists of grass and dirt tracks. We strongly recommend bringing an ergonomic baby carrier or sling for walking on the island rather than a wheeled stroller.
Best Departure Window: Aim for 08:30 AM to 10:00 AM. The water is glassy and calm, avoiding any bumpy afternoon chop that might unnerve younger children.
Sun & Hydration: Bring sun hats with tie straps (lake breezes love to blow loose hats into the water) and a favorite water bottle or juice box for the ride.
Restroom Availability: Restrooms are available at Karagita Beach before departure, and basic eco-toilets are available at the Crescent Island landing gate.
