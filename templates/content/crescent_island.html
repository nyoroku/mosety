What is Crescent Island?

Crescent Island is a submerged volcanic crater rim located on the eastern basin of Lake Naivasha. Because it forms a protected peninsula connected only by a shallow causeway during dry periods, wild herbivore populations flourish here in extraordinary safety.

There are no lions, leopards, hyenas, or cheetahs on the island. Because of this absence of apex predators, the resident giraffes, zebras, and antelopes are remarkably calm, allowing visitors to walk within 15–20 meters of grazing herds accompanied by sanctuary guides.

Financial Transparency

Understanding the Two Separate Costs

A common point of confusion for first-time visitors to Naivasha is the relationship between boat charter fees and sanctuary entrance fees. These are two distinct operational costs paid to different entities:

1. Boat Ride (Paid to Mosety)
KES 3,500 – 4,500

Covers round-trip boat charter for your entire group (up to 7 guests), licensed captain, fuel, life jackets, and hippo pod viewing en route.

2. Sanctuary Gate Fee (Paid at Gate)
KES 1,000 / $33 USD

Paid directly to Crescent Island Sanctuary management via M-Pesa or Card. Citizens/Residents: KES 1,000 (child KES 500). Non-residents: $33 USD (child $16).

Mosety dock promise: We never bundle inflated entrance gate markups into boat rates. You pay official gate tariffs directly to sanctuary rangers at the landing dock.

The 3-Step Crescent Island Experience

1

Boat Departure & Hippo Observation (20–25 Mins)

Board your boat at Karagita Beach. Your captain navigates along protected acacia shorelines where resident hippo pods rest in the shallows. Observe hippos at ethical 30–50 meter buffers and watch African fish eagles perched above.

2

Guided Walking Safari (60–90 Mins)

Disembark at the Crescent Island jetty. Complete park entry payment at the ranger hut. A sanctuary guide escorts your party on a loop trail across open grassland, getting within close proximity of Maasai giraffes, Grant's gazelles, and defassa waterbucks.

3

Scenic Return Cruise (15–20 Mins)

Your Mosety captain waits at the dock while you walk. Board for a gentle return cruise with scenic vistas across the Mau Escarpment and Mount Longonot in the background.

Wildlife Checklist on Crescent Island

Maasai Giraffes Tallest wild population in the area, grazing quietly from yellow-fever acacias.
Burchell's Zebras Large family herds with foals grazing alongside wildebeests.
Defassa Waterbucks Shaggy, majestic antelopes inhabiting the shoreline reed zones.
African Fish Eagles Resident pairs nesting atop ancient dead acacia snags along the water.

Best Departure Time for Crescent Island

We recommend departing between 08:30 AM and 10:00 AM. During these mid-morning hours, Lake Naivasha is at its calmest, temperatures are pleasantly crisp for walking, and animals are actively feeding in open clearings before midday heat sets in.

Afternoon visits (after 14:30) can also be rewarding for golden lighting, but afternoon lake chop can make the boat return slightly bumpy.
