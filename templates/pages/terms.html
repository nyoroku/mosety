Lake Naivasha Charter & Booking Policies • September 2026

1. Boat Charters & Pier Rates

All Mosety boat safaris depart from Karagita Public Pier, South Lake Road, Naivasha. Rates published on this website reflect standard direct dock prices including the complete boat, licensed captain, required marine fuel, and fitted life jackets for all passengers.

2. Free Cancellation & Rescheduling

We maintain a flexible cancellation policy. You may reschedule or cancel your boat ride up to 2 hours prior to scheduled departure with zero cancellation fee. If weather conditions on Lake Naivasha become adverse (heavy afternoon squalls or rough swell), trips will be adjusted to sheltered waters or rescheduled at no cost for passenger safety.

3. Passenger Safety & Life Jackets

In accordance with Kenya Maritime Authority safety protocols, certified life vests are mandatory for all passengers during the entire duration on the water. Specialized junior safety collars are provided for toddlers and infants. Passengers must follow the safety instructions of Captain Moses or Captain Joseph at all times.

4. Wildlife Observation Buffer Zones

Our captains maintain strict ethical distances (30 to 50 meters) from wild hippopotamus pods and nesting waterbirds. Under no circumstances will vessels pursue or corner wildlife.

5. Third-Party Sanctuary Fees (Crescent Island)

Boat charter pricing covers water transit and captain waiting time. Separate sanctuary admission fees for Crescent Island Game Sanctuary (payable directly to the sanctuary gate) are not included in the boat charter fee unless explicitly stated in a custom package.
