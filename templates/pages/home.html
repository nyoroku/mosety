How much is a boat ride at Lake Naivasha?

Private 1-hour boat rides start at KES 3,500 per boat (up to 7 guests). Crescent Island transfers are KES 5,000/boat, sunset cruises KES 6,000/boat, and group rides start at KES 1,000/person.

Open 2026 Price Calculator →

What are typical boat ride times?

Day boat rides depart daily from 06:30 AM to 06:30 PM. Standard wildlife rides last 1 hour, sunset cruises run 1.5 hours (around 4:30 PM), and Crescent Island boat + walk trips take 2.5–3 hours.

Best Departure Times →

Is there an entry fee for Lake Naivasha?

No. There is no general KWS park entry fee to access Lake Naivasha or board at Karagita Public Pier. Separate gate fees apply only if you walk on Crescent Island Sanctuary (KES 800 citizen / KES 1,100 resident / $33 USD non-resident).

Read Entry Fee Guide →

How much does it cost to go to Naivasha?

A full day trip from Nairobi (matatu KES 400–600 each way or ~90-minute drive, boat safari, optional Crescent Island walk, and lakeside lunch) averages KES 3,000–6,500 for Kenyan residents or $75–$130 USD for international visitors.

Living Wetland

The lake is never the same twice.

Lake Naivasha is a dynamic freshwater ecosystem supporting over 1,500 wild hippopotamuses and 400 species of resident and migratory birds. While nature offers no guarantees, quiet morning waters provide exceptional viewing opportunities.

Destination Highlight

Crescent Island boat ride & walking safari.

Combine a scenic Lake Naivasha boat ride with an unhurried walking safari among wild giraffes, zebras, and wildebeests on Crescent Island Game Sanctuary. A signature walking safari adventure on Lake Naivasha for families, couples, and photographers.

How the Lake Naivasha Boat Ride & Crescent Island Trip works:

  • Boat charter (KES 5,000/boat): 15–25 minute scenic cruise from Karagita Pier with hippo pod viewing en route.
  • Waiting time included: Your boat and captain wait at the island jetty while you take a 1.5–2 hour guided walking safari.
  • 2026 Sanctuary gate fee (paid separately): Kenya Citizens KES 800 adult (KES 400 child), Kenya Residents KES 1,100 adult (KES 550 child), Non-Residents $33 USD adult ($22 student, $16 child; under 5 free).

Operational Rigor

Maritime safety standards on Lake Naivasha.

Safety on the water is our highest priority. Every Lake Naivasha boat safari departure is led by certified captains with strict 30–50m hippo buffer zones, mandatory life vests for all ages, and calm-water navigation protocols.

01. Pre-Trip Briefing

Your captain explains water conditions, seating balance, and hippo safety rules before pushing off from the pier.

02. Fitted Life Jackets

Mandatory certified vests for every guest, including specialized junior safety collars for infants and toddlers.

03. Weather Decisions

We monitor afternoon wind shifts. If waters turn rough, we adjust routes to sheltered bays or reschedule at zero cost.

04. Strict Capacity

We never overload vessels. Standard private charters cap at 7 guests per boat for roll stability and comfortable views.
