Destination Highlight

Crescent Island boat ride & walking safari.

Combine a scenic Lake Naivasha boat ride with an unhurried walking safari among wild giraffes, zebras, and wildebeests on Crescent Island Game Sanctuary. Widely rated as the best boat ride Naivasha adventure for families, couples, and photographers.

How the Mosety Crescent Island Trip works:

  • Boat charter: 25-minute scenic cruise from Karagita pier to the island.
  • Waiting time: Your boat and captain wait at the pier while you take your 1.5 - 2 hour walking tour.
  • Separate fee note: Sanctuary entry fees (KES 1,000 resident / $33 non-resident) are paid directly to the sanctuary gate upon arrival.

Operational Rigor

The best boat ride Naivasha safety standards.

Safety on the water is our highest priority. Every Lake Naivasha boat safari departure is led by certified captains with strict 30–50m hippo buffer zones, mandatory life vests for all ages, and calm-water navigation protocols.

01. Pre-Trip Briefing

Your captain explains water conditions, seating balance, and hippo safety rules before pushing off from the pier.

02. Fitted Life Jackets

Mandatory certified vests for every guest, including specialized junior safety collars for infants and toddlers.

03. Weather Decisions

We monitor afternoon wind shifts. If waters turn rough, we adjust routes to sheltered bays or reschedule at zero cost.

04. Strict Capacity

We never overload vessels. Standard private charters cap at 7 guests per boat for roll stability and comfortable views.
