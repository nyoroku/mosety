Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 • Mosety Boat Rides Naivasha

1. Information We Collect

When you make an inquiry or reservation with Mosety Boat Rides Naivasha (via website forms, WhatsApp, phone, or direct pier check-in), we may collect your name, phone number, email address, requested safari date, party size, and any special accessibility requirements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your details exclusively to coordinate your Lake Naivasha boat charter, provide gate directions to Karagita Pier, share real-time weather or water safety updates, and deliver booking confirmations. We do not sell, rent, or trade guest personal information to third-party marketing companies.

3. Direct WhatsApp & Communication

Our primary reservation channel operates via official WhatsApp dispatch (+254 114 182706). Message history is retained solely for operational dispatch, passenger safety logs required by maritime authorities, and customer service continuity.

4. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses minimal, anonymized telemetry (e.g. Google Analytics 4) to understand site performance, general traffic sources, and currency switcher preferences. No personally identifiable financial data is tracked or stored.

5. Contact Regarding Privacy

For questions or data deletion requests, contact Mosety Boat Rides Naivasha directly at Karagita Public Pier, South Lake Road, Naivasha, Kenya, or via WhatsApp at +254 114 182706.
